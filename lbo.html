🔑 Investment Thesis

India Healthcare Growth

Healthcare spend growing 15–18% p.a., underpenetrated vs global peers; rising insurance coverage

Premium Brand Moat

Medanta recognised as North India's top quaternary care provider with strong physician ecosystem

Capacity Expansion

1,000+ new beds planned across Tier-1 & Tier-2 cities; greenfield + brownfield pipeline

Margin Expansion

Operating leverage as occupancy rises; premium procedure mix shift towards oncology, cardiac & neuro

Technology Edge

Robotics, AI-assisted diagnostics & digital health positioning vs regional competitors

Exit Optionality

IPO, strategic sale or secondary to global healthcare PE at sustained premium multiple
